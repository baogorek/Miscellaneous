Art Show
===========

It's time to run an ASCII art contest! In parallel, artists @agent-ascii-artist-rzr , @agent-ascii-artist-vivienne , and 
  @agent-ascii-mad-max will create their ASCII art. Then when all three are done, the critic @agent-ascii-art-critic will sequentially display
   the art, grade the art, and, when all pieces have been graded, he will announce the winner and show the art piece one more time. Under the displayed artwork, the full set of scoring criteria will appear. Note that the critic is creating his own .txt file, along with the artists.

At the end, the user might say something like, "Show's over, let's clean up." And then (and only then), will you delete the .txt files.
